# Networking System: Global Remote Outreach
**Goal:** Bypass the ATS black hole by generating warm referrals and direct conversations with hiring managers, founders, and engineers.

---

## 1. High-Priority Target Contacts
When applying to roles on Otta, Wellfound, or YC Jobs, identify contacts in this priority sequence:
1.  **Founder / co-founder** (for startups with < 30 employees).
2.  **Engineering Manager / VP of Engineering** (for mid-sized remote companies, 30–150 employees).
3.  **Senior Software Engineers** on the team you are applying to.
4.  **Amity University Alumni** working in global remote roles or target locations (US, UK, EU, SG, AU).

## 4. The Warm Referral Playbook
If the conversation goes well during an informational interview:
1.  **Do not ask for a job directly.**
2.  **Ask for advice on their team's pipeline:** *"I saw a Software Engineer role open on your team. Given what we discussed about developer tools, do you have any advice on what the hiring manager is looking for in that specific role?"*
3.  **Offer a referral request:** If they respond positively, ask: *"Would you be open to introducing me to the hiring manager or passing along my resume? I'd love to make sure my application highlights the right system engineering focus."*
