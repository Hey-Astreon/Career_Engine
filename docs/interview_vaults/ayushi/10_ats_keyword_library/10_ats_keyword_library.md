## 2. Dynamic Action Verbs (C-A-R-S Standard)
Use these to replace passive phrasing like "responsible for" or "helped with":

*   **For System Architecture:** `Architected`, `Engineered`, `Designed`, `Pioneered`, `Orchestrated`, `Structured`.
*   **For Optimization:** `Optimized`, `Accelerated`, `Reduced`, `Minimized`, `Scaled`, `Streamlined`, `Refactored`.
*   **For Integration:** `Integrated`, `Embedded`, `Wired`, `Multiplexed`, `Connected`, `Bridged`.
*   **For Verification:** `Authored`, `Tested`, `Validated`, `Secured`, `Audited`, `Intercepted`.
